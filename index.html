Reading the hotel-size codes

Suppliers write these as a short code. Every hotel pan is spelt out in full on its card:

37F – 50 / 9 Kg

37 = number of pits (how many appams at once)
F = flat base  ·  K = kuzhi, the rounded bump base
50 = width across each pit, in millimetres
9 Kg = weight of the pan itself

Regular appam and paniyaram pans are measured differently — those show the width of the whole pan and the thickness of the metal.

Tap any product to open its full card — every size it comes in, the rate, the MRP and the minimum order quantity, plus more photos where we have them.

How to use this catalogue
🔎

Search by whatever you call it

Type the local name or the catalogue name — both work. kadhai finds Vadachatti, appam finds Paniyarakal, kiwi tawa finds Addakal.

Brand names work too. Paxton shows the whole Paxton range; Paxton tawa narrows it to tawas. You can also search a product code like MZ-157.

🎚️

Narrowing things down

The row of chips under the search box jumps straight to a category. Filters opens the full set — category, brand and type together. The number on the Filters button tells you how many are active.

📦

Opening a product

Tap any product to see every size with its own rate, MRP where the supplier prints one, and all its photos. Tap a photo to enlarge it.

Enquire on WhatsApp sends us the product and its sizes directly. Share product gives you a link you can send to anyone — it opens as a proper product card in WhatsApp.

⬇️

Saving a PDF

The download button at the top exports whatever is on screen right now — so filter to one brand or category first if that's all you need.

Price list puts every size on its own line, best for checking rates. Showcase uses large photos, better for sending to a customer. You can group by category or by brand, and turn prices, MRP and thumbnails on or off.

📖

Hotel-size codes

Codes like 37F — 50 / 9 Kg mean 37 pits, flat base, each pit 50 mm across, pan weighs 9 kg. K instead of F means kuzhi — the rounded bump base. Every hotel pan spells this out in full on its card.

💬

Prices and ordering

Rates shown include GST. Some brands are still being priced up — those show a dash for now. Ordering happens over WhatsApp, not on this site.
